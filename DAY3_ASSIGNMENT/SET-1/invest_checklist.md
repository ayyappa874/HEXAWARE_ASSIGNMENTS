# INVEST checklist

| INVEST element | What it means | How it is applied to this backlog |
|---|---|---|
| Independent | A story should have as few dependencies on other stories as practical. | Stories are grouped by feature area; dependencies such as checkout requiring cart contents are identified during refinement. |
| Negotiable | A story describes an outcome, not a fixed technical solution. | The stories state customer needs and leave implementation choices to the Developers. |
| Valuable | A story delivers value to a user or stakeholder. | Each story explains why the customer needs the capability. |
| Estimable | The team has enough information to estimate the effort. | Descriptions, acceptance criteria and known dependencies are clarified before estimation. |
| Small | A story is small enough to complete within a sprint. | Large end-to-end capabilities are split into focused stories such as cart quantity, cart total and checkout. |
| Testable | There are clear conditions for verifying completion. | Acceptance criteria are written in observable Given/When/Then form. |
